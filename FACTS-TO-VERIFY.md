## 1. Entry & visas (highest priority)

| # | Claim on the site | Where it appears | Verify with |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Switzerland is part of the Schengen Area but not the EU, and applies common Schengen visa rules. | Travel Tips → Entry & visas; Home FAQ | sem.admin.ch (State Secretariat for Migration) |
| E2 | Many nationalities (examples given: **US, Canada, UK, Australia**) can usually stay **up to 90 days in any 180-day period** without a visa. | Travel Tips → Entry & visas; Home FAQ | SEM visa list; EU Schengen calculator |
| E3 | Non-EU/EFTA visitors generally need a passport **valid at least 3 months beyond departure** from Schengen and **issued within the last 10 years**. | Travel Tips → Entry & visas | SEM; your nearest Swiss embassy |

| E5 | **ETIAS** (pre-travel authorisation for visa-exempt travellers) is *planned*. The site gives no start date on purpose. Check whether it has started and whether it applies to Switzerland. | Travel Tips → Entry & visas; Home FAQ | travel-europe.europa.eu/etias; SEM |
| E6 | Travel insurance covering mountain rescue and repatriation is *required by Swiss Horizon*. This is our booking policy, not a legal entry rule. Keep it as company policy or reword. | Travel Tips; Home "Not included" | Your booking terms |
